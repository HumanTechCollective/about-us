# Carmen Bermejo Hernández

Software developer, AI Engineer and community builder based in Madrid, Spain.

## Background

Carmen has a degree in Computer Systems Engineering and a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence from the Polytechnic University of Madrid (UPM). Her master's thesis explored how discussion topics emerge and opinions spread in online communities, using NLP and machine learning techniques to analyze public debate on the Spanish platform [Menéame](https://www.meneame.net/).

She is especially interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence and social sciences: understanding how ideas flow, how public opinion forms and shifts, and what role technology plays in these dynamics.

## Professional experience

Carmen's career spans software development, AI engineering and product management, mostly in the startup world. She has built AI-powered tools including text classifiers, information extraction systems and a RAG chatbot. As a product manager at Nextail, she led the development of optimization algorithms for retail supply chains.

Beyond technical roles, Carmen has spent over a decade building communities and organizations in the Spanish and European startup ecosystems:

- She co-founded the **Tetuan Valley Association** in 2013 to help the Tetuan Valley Startup School (a pre-accelerator program founded in 2009) stand on its own as an organization. As president and later vice president, she led the association for over a decade, organizing programs across multiple cities in Spain, mentoring hundreds of founders and building a thriving alumni and mentor community. She also launched the Deep Tech Sessions, a series of talks on AI.

- She co-founded the **Spanish Startups Association** and served as its president. Under her leadership, the association helped bring a cross-party non-legislative proposition to the Spanish Congress that laid the groundwork for Spain's Startup Law, approved in 2022.

- She served on the board of the **European Startup Network** and the continental council of **Allied for Startups**, contributing to startup policy at the European level.

## What drives her

Carmen is passionate about using technology to understand and improve society. Throughout her career, whether mentoring entrepreneurs, building AI tools or advocating for startup-friendly policy, she has been driven by a desire to create things that matter and to help others do the same.
